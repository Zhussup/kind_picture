# Legitimacy snapshots

Verbatim English-language snapshots of the licenses and terms that govern
building, training and publishing `kind_picture`. Retrieved on **2026-10-21**.

The files are kept in their original form: license files are raw text, web
pages are the raw HTML response bodies. HTML snapshots include site
navigation and scripts; the source URL remains the authoritative version.

> This directory is a record of the terms at the retrieval date. It is not
> legal advice. Re-check the source URLs before a public release.

## Key excerpts

### Beam license

Ollama blog, "Reflection's Beam is freely available on Ollama for a limited
time":

> Beam is released under Apache 2.0, giving developers the freedom to inspect,
> customize, and build on the model.

> In collaboration with Reflection, Ollama's Beam endpoint runs with full zero
> data retention (ZDR).

### Beam outputs and distillation

Ollama model page for `beam:cloud`:

> Reflection does not prohibit distillation, use of outputs to train other
> models, fine-tuning, parameter-efficient adaptation, quantization, merging,
> defensive security research on systems you are authorized to test, and
> good-faith adversarial evaluation of the model.

### Beam provenance rule

Reflection Usage Policy:

> remove, alter, obscure, or degrade any watermark, label, metadata, or other
> provenance signal that we apply to our models or their output, or distribute
> output with those signals stripped or falsified

This is why the raw shards in `ml/asm/raw_c/` keep the complete listings. The
dataset builder removes only `# prog.c:7: ...` source-line comments produced by
`gcc -fverbose-asm`; that is an anti-leak transformation for training, not a
provider watermark.

### DeepSeek outputs and distillation

DeepSeek Terms of Use, Section 4.2(3):

> You may apply the Inputs and Outputs of the Services to a wide range of use
> cases, including personal use, academic research, derivative product
> development, training other models (such as model distillation), etc., as
> long as such usage is legal and adhere to these Terms.

### DeepSeek AI-content labelling

DeepSeek Terms of Use:

> If you publish or disseminate outputs generated by the Services, you must:
> (1) proactively verify the authenticity and accuracy of the output content
> to avoid spreading false information; (2) clearly indicate that the output
> content is generated by artificial intelligence, to alert the public to the
> fact that the content is generated by AI.

### Ollama ownership and restrictions

Ollama Terms of Service, May 2026:

> We claim no ownership over your inputs or outputs.

> Use our services to develop competing products

> Use automated means to access our services without permission

Ollama documents programmatic access through `ollama signin` and the local API,
and through `https://ollama.com/api` with an `Authorization: Bearer` API key.
The collector uses the documented local API, one request at a time, with
backoff and jitter. The "competing products" clause should be read against the
narrow asm-to-C purpose of this project; document that purpose in any public
model card.

### Base model

Qwen3-0.6B is Apache 2.0 (Copyright 2024 Alibaba Cloud). The repository-level
license is in `../LICENSE.md`.

## Practical consequences

- Training on Beam or DeepSeek outputs is permitted by both providers.
- The trained adapter or merged model may be published, provided the Qwen3
  Apache 2.0 notice is retained and the model card discloses the AI-generated
  training data and the teacher models.
- Do not strip provider watermarks or provenance metadata.
- Keep the raw shards, because they are the provenance record for the
  leak-stripped training data.
- Use the documented API and stay within the plan's concurrency and rate
  limits. Do not create extra accounts to bypass limits.
- DeepSeek's hosted service is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of
  China; verify that your jurisdiction and plan are eligible.
- The free Beam access is limited in time. Already generated outputs remain
  usable under the terms recorded here, but indefinite free collection is not
  guaranteed.

## Verification

`manifest.json` contains the retrieval timestamp and the SHA-256 of every file
in this directory. To verify a snapshot:

```bash
sha256sum -c <(python3 -c '
import json
for row in json.load(open("manifest.json"))["files"]:
    print(row["sha256"] + "  " + row["file"])
')
```

Run that command from this directory.
